✳FAQ

What you need to know before we cook

How far in advance should I book?

Two to four weeks for private dinners, and two to three months for weddings and large events. Short notice is sometimes possible, so just ask.

Can you cook for dietary requirements?

Yes. Halal, gluten-free, dairy-free, vegetarian and allergy-aware menus are planned from the start, never as an afterthought.

Do you travel outside the Maldives?

Yes, for private events and consulting projects. Travel and accommodation are quoted separately.

What is included in a private dinner?

Menu design, shopping, cooking, service and cleanup. Wine pairing and extra service staff can be added.

How many guests can you cater for?

From an intimate dinner for two up to a few hundred guests with the full brigade.

Not sure where to start?

A short call to understand the occasion, the guests and what it really needs.
